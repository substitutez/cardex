Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 9, 2026 · CarDex Production Build v2.4

📷 1. Camera Feed & Optical Recognition

CarDex utilizes device camera hardware solely for the live optical identification of automotive make, model, chassis generation, paint finish, and trim options.

  • In-Memory Processing: Captured frames are streamed ephemerally in RAM to our multimodal vision model for real-time inference.
  • No Indiscriminate Storage: Raw image frames are discarded immediately after analysis unless you explicitly log the vehicle to your personal Spotter Garage.
  • Facial & License Plate Obfuscation: CarDex algorithms focus exclusively on automotive sheet metal and emblems. We do not index human faces or personal identifying attributes.

🎤 2. Microphone & Acoustic Exhaust Telemetry

When launching the Acoustic Dyno or rev analyzer, CarDex requests access to the device microphone to perform IEC 61672-compliant sound level metering.

  • Local DSP Measurement: Audio buffers are processed via fast Fourier transform (FFT) and 1/3-octave band filtering to calculate peak A-weighted decibels (dBA) and dominant harmonic frequencies.
  • No Voice Recording: The audio analysis pipeline strictly measures sound pressure and spectral cadence. CarDex does not transcribe, record, or retain human speech or ambient conversations.
  • Immediate Buffer Purge: Audio buffers are discarded from device memory as soon as the acoustic verification badge is computed.

📍 3. Location & Spatial Quantization (Anti-Doxxing)

To enable the Live Radar Hotspot Map and localized supercar sighting alerts, CarDex accesses device location with strict privacy safeguards:

Zero Precision GPS Storage: CarDex quantizes all coordinates using a standard 500-meter fuzzy geohash radius. We NEVER store or broadcast raw pinpoint GPS coordinates to prevent doxxing private residences or secure car collections.
  • Spotting markers visible to other users indicate general sectors (e.g., "Monaco Harbour Sector" or "Rodeo Dr Hotspot").
  • You may disable location permissions in your operating system settings at any time; offline and manual spotting features remain fully functional without GPS.

💳 4. In-App Purchases, Quotas & Telemetry

CarDex uses Apple StoreKit and Stripe to process subscriptions (CarDex Pro) and consumable fuel refill packs.

  • Payment Security: All financial transactions are handled directly by Apple or Stripe. CarDex servers never see, store, or transmit your credit card numbers.
  • Account State: We store only your spotter username, current score, daily scan quota, and subscription entitlement status in encrypted Firestore databases.

🛡️ 5. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You have full ownership of your spotting garage. You may request immediate deletion of your spotter profile, accumulated points, and saved sightings by contacting our team at privacy@cardex.app.
